# Hart Sales OS — New Rep Quick Start

*One page. Read it once, then take the in-app tour: **Quick Guide → "Learn Hart Sales OS"**.*

---

## What this tool is

Hart Sales OS answers four questions, every day, in this order:

1. **Who do I need to see?** → Your **Dashboard** and **My Route**
2. **Who am I meeting?** → Follow-up dates you set become your schedule
3. **What happened?** → **Log Activity** (30 seconds, voice or tap)
4. **What's next?** → The follow-up date + Next Step on every contact

If you log your visits and set follow-ups, the system runs your day for you.
If you don't, your manager sees that too — the Alerts page shows reps with no
activity in 3 days.

## Day one — 10 minutes

1. **Sign in** with the email and password from your manager
   (bottom of the sidebar → *Change password* to set your own).
2. **Take the tour** — the orange banner on your dashboard, or Quick Guide →
   *Learn Hart Sales OS*. Two minutes, on the real screens.
3. **Add your first contacts** — My Contacts → *Add contact*. Name, company,
   type. A COI ("Center of Influence") is anyone who can send us work: insurance
   agents, plumbers, property managers, adjusters, realtors.

## The daily loop

**Morning:** open the Dashboard. Overdue names first, then due today. That's
your hit list — My Route sorts it into a driving order with tap-to-call.

**After every visit** (in the parking lot, before you drive off), log it one of
two ways:

- **Quick Log** — the orange **+** button. Contact, type, note, follow-up date.
  Four taps.
- **Voice note** — *Log Activity* in the menu. Talk like a human:
  > "Just met Sarah Chen at Westside Property Management. Category 3 water
  > loss in the basement. Sending a $12,400 estimate. Follow up Thursday at 9."
  The AI turns that into a CRM record — contact, damage type, dollar value,
  follow-up date — and shows it to you before saving. Say **Who, What, When**
  and your confidence score stays high; leave one out and the note gets
  flagged for review.

**The follow-up date is the whole system.** Whatever date you set is when that
contact reappears on your dashboard. Never leave a visit without one.

## Three rules

1. **Log it the same day.** An unlogged visit didn't happen.
2. **Always set the follow-up.** "What's next" is the point of the CRM.
3. **Dollar amounts belong in the note.** Mention the estimate value and the
   deal appears in your pipeline automatically.
